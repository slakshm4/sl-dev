contextomy.

a study in subtraction

Every page conceals a poem. Begin with Dickinson, Shelley or Thoreau, or with words of your own, and strike out whatever doesn't belong. What remains is yours to set on paper of your choosing and keep as a print or a short film.

contextomy
/kənˈtɛkst.ə.mi/ · noun

The extraction of words from their original context in a way that alters their meaning.

Doubt not, O poet, but persist. Say, ‘It is in me, and shall out.’struck from Ralph Waldo Emerson, The Poet (1844)

contextomy. is a blackout poetry studio for erasure, redaction and found poetry, drawn from public-domain literature or your own writing. Free to use. Your poems never leave your browser.
